Dessert Recipes

End your meal with something sweet and delightful!

Chocolate Cake

  • Flour
  • Cocoa powder
  • Sugar, eggs, butter

Mix all ingredients, pour into a pan, and bake for 30 minutes at 180°C. Serve with melted chocolate.

Fruit Salad

  • Apple
  • Banana
  • Orange, honey

Chop fruits into small pieces, drizzle with honey, and chill for 15 minutes before serving.
