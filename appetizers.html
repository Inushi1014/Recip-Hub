Appetizer Recipes

Start your meal with something tasty and irresistible!

Garlic Bread

  • Bread slices
  • Butter
  • Garlic, parsley

Mix butter and garlic, spread on bread, and bake for 10 minutes until golden.

Tomato Bruschetta

  • Baguette
  • Tomatoes
  • Olive oil, basil

Toast bread, top with chopped tomatoes, olive oil, and basil for a fresh bite.

Stuffed Mushrooms

  • Button mushrooms
  • Cream cheese
  • Garlic, breadcrumbs

Fill mushroom caps with a mix of cream cheese, garlic, and breadcrumbs, then bake for 15 minutes.
